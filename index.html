The problem

Monitoring is not evidence.

Ratios you can't verify

Performance ratios inherit whatever the sensors say. A drifting pyranometer rewrites a site's reputation for years, and nothing flags it.

Outages nobody attributes

Production stops. Whose side of the meter failed, and who pays for it, never enters the record. The loss quietly becomes yours.

Records that fail diligence

At refinancing and exit, an unverifiable operating history is priced against you. The caveat costs more than the kilowatt-hours.

What we do

Three levels of proof. Each stands alone.

LEVEL 1FROM YOUR EXISTING DATA

Forensic screen

Verdicts from the data your fleet already produces: which records can be trusted, every detected stoppage attributed to its side of the meter, and a disposition per site, with no hardware and no site visits.

LEVEL 2BY INSTRUMENT

Instrumented site audit

It starts with the site’s own records: a year of inverter alarms, unit-against-unit comparisons and sensor checks, before any hardware ships. Where the array itself is in doubt, we name the strings to test. The truth of a site, established once, at instrument grade.

The record stays current, so the performance number you hand a lender, an auditor, or a buyer comes without the data-quality caveat.

Why was Site 07 down on June 14?

Site 07 was offline for 2 hours 45 minutes on June 14, from 14:02 to 16:47 EDT. The outage began at or above the site's collection bus, and the record places the origin beyond the meter.

All six inverters lost AC voltage within the same second E-114. Independently fed inverters do not fail together, so the fault sits at the shared bus or upstream of it. The onset registers carry grid-observation bits in the first sampled interval E-115, which places it upstream rather than on the site side, and the revenue meter recorded no export for the whole window E-116.

The monitoring platform logged no downtime that day E-117. Against the irradiance at the time E-118, lost generation is 2.1 MWh, upper bound 2.4 MWh. None of it is booked anywhere yet.

Attributed · Utility Meter verified Not in site record
What this cannot show Why the utility feeder tripped. That record sits with the utility. Trackable can establish which side of the meter the fault came from, not what the utility's equipment did.
